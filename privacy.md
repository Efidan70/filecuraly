# Filecuraly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Filecuraly is a file organizer app made by nimoo's studio. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## In short

Filecuraly does **not** collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything happens on your own device.
There is no account, no internet connection, no advertising and no analytics.

## What the app accesses

To move files between folders (for example from Download to Documents), Filecuraly asks for **All files access** on Android. It uses this access only to:

- list the files in the folder you choose,
- read file names, types, sizes and dates to decide where a file belongs,
- read file contents only to find exact duplicates, by comparing checksums on the device,
- move files into folders, and move them back when you tap Undo.

Files are never deleted by the app. Duplicates you choose to remove are moved to a "Filecuraly Trash" folder on your device, so they can be restored.

## What is stored, and where

Your settings, your own folder rules and the history of changes (used for Undo) are stored only inside the app on your device.
They are removed when you uninstall the app. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

## Sharing

No data is shared with third parties.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
